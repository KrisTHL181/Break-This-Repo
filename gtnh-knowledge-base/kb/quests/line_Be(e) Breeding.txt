### [-9136162451931116134] Attuned
You're now getting into Magic Bees, and one of its mundane bee species is the Attuned species. These are scooped out of Deep Hives that you can find in the Overworld. These hives are a pain to find, so you should consider just buying hives with coins until you get a princess and a drone. However, if you are a real masochist or completionist try-hard, then here's some information about where they spawn:  * From Y=1 to Y=25 * In stone, surrounded on 5 sides. Not just vanilla stone. * In biomes tagged with one or more of:    * MOUNTAINS: Actual mountains may not count. It's the biome type that counts.       * Example: Canyon.    * HILLS: Again, actual hills don't count.       * Example: Snowy Coniferous Forest.    * MAGICAL: Unlike the others, they do have some ethereal aspect.       * Examples: Magical Forest is obvious, but also Cherry Blossom Grove counts. * Surrounded by a small redstone vein. This often fills any open space around them.  They also like to just kind of show up whatever-what-the-heck in other biomes. Our beekeeping intern found a Deep Hive in a Lush Desert:  Lush Desert [HOT, DRY, SAVANNA, LUSH, SANDY]  You can dump the biome data in NEI by clicking on the NEI options button (the wrench), then the Tools button, then the Data Dumps button, and finally the Dump button in the biomes category. This creates a "dumps" directory in the root folder of the modpack with a biome.csv inside of it. Any listed biome that has the tags of HILLS, MOUNTAINS, and/or MAGICAL can generate Deep Hives. You can feed these biomes into a Nature's Compass and see if there are any nearby.  Prospector's Scanners will pick up the little ore pockets for these (traces, small samples). If you had one of those end-game ones with a display, it would come up as a few red pixels. Larger ones are probably just regular ore veins. The ore finding wand doesn't do a good job from the surface. Chasing lava veins and tunnels helps. Ore veins filled with redstone will saturate either tool.  Of course, just buying hives is the low-effort way.  [note]Beekeeper's Notes:  If you get real bad luck and wind up with an ignoble princess, then just get a bunch of drones out of it and convert some other pristine princess afterwards. However, using base princesses will compel the bees to turn into the Common species. There are two choices:  1. Use an Alveary with an Alveary Stabilizer to block mutations. 2. Create a princess that doesn't line up with the Attuned species' breeding possibilities before starting.[/note]

### [1117] Breed some common bees
To cross breed bees you need two different species of bees. One drone and one princess from different naturally-generating hives (Meadows and Forest as an example).  Bee Houses do not allow crossbreeding. You need an Apiary to cross-breed them to a new species. Certain frames will help this process. Shortening their lifespan means faster crosses. Increasing the mutation rate means getting mutations and thus rare bees quicker.  Common Bees produce Honey Combs every 2.2 minutes. Note that production times are an approximation based on the bee's base production speed and the product's rarity. They do not reflect actual production time.  Make sure your bees are pure by checking their genetics with a Beealyzer or GT scanner.  [note]Breeder's Notes:  Riiiight from the start, they have a trick for you. The Common bee is bred from most mundane species smashed into each other. The problem is the Cultivated species, which is next in line, is bred with most mundane species along with Common. Noble and Diligent then are crossed from Common and Cultivated. You could start with two mundane bees, have the results loop back unattended, and run that whole progression. Here's an example active:inactive mix you could get on a princess over time:  Meadows:Forest Meadows:Common (Yay!) Common:Meadows (Double yay! Active trait!) Cultivated:Common (Boo!) . . . some time passes . . . Diligent:Noble (Wait, what?!)  Meanwhile, this quest would still be sitting there, waiting for you to submit Common bees. Whoops!  If Cultivated starts to show up, just stuff any princesses and drones involved into a chest and act like that didn't happen for now. You can use them when you complete this quest to jumpstart the Cultivated species.  Now, if you don't have any way to speed up breeding and don't want to stand around to micromanage, you can try filtering, or just checking on the result from time to time. Either way, maybe don't leave it going overnight if you're on a server.[/note]

### [1116] Breed some cultivated bees
Common Princess and Forest Drone can be bred to cultivated bees.  Cultivated bees have the shortest lifespan - this means they make great princesses to breed with your newly bred drones. The queen will die quickly, quickly giving genetic material to work with. Make sure the traits you want are kept!  They are also a Fast production bee, which means lots of honey combs for powering your Beealyzer or Treealyzer.  Cultivated Bees produce Honey Combs every 0.9 minutes. Note that production times are an approximation based on the bee's base production speed and the product's rarity. They do not reflect actual production time.  Make sure your cultivated bees are pure with a Beealyzer or GT scanner.  The main bee breeding quests will require pure bees but you can use Ignoble or Pristine princesses.

### [2168] Coal
Ah yes, Coal is King indeed, but Coal Bees are matriarchal. A great bee, giving you Coal to use to power your industry. This will require a Coal Block under your bee housing. This bee will also have the first really dangerous effect - it will apply a creeper explosion to you if you are not wearing your Apiarist suit. Breed them from Peat and Industrious. Consider using some lifetime-reducing frames to accelerate that so you can get a Coal drone stock more quickly and start on those other species.[/note]

Breed them from Ethereal and Infernal.  [note]Astronomer's Notes: The waxing and waning phases happen on alternating days. Also, congratulations if you just learned that the moon in Minecraft has phases!  Breeder's Notes: Just because this bee only mutates around specific lunar phases doesn't mean you can't work on them in other phases. To get the mutations to show up at all, yes, you need correct lunar phases. However, you will need a spare Infernal or Ethereal princess to get started, and you can prep that any day. Also, once the Vis species trait shows up in some drones and/or a princess, it's just regular cross-breeding to get a pure princess and pure drone. That can be done any day too.  Despite the focus on moon phases, you do not need to breed at night to trigger the mutation.  The Ethereal and Infernal bees are a rough combination. Since you'll need a new princess for this bee, consider using a pristine princess that isn't aggressive and is closer to your Ethereal bees' climate. If you get lucky, you may breed out the aggression and produce hybrid Infernals that don't need to live in an oven to function. You will probably need your apiarist suit if the bred queen gets a dominant Infernal trait for a few cycles.[/note]

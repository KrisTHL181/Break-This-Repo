### [1654] §6§lAutomating Power On/Off
Once you have a GT boiler or Large Combustion Engine with a long startup time, you may wish to automate turning it on/off to save fuel, since extra power or Steam will just be voided. Additionally, the bigger your Steam/EU buffer is, the less time your generators will spend heating up, which makes them more efficient.  Use a Machine Controller Cover on the controller for that. You can put it on top, bottom, or sides next to a casing - casings will transmit the redstone signal. You can use a Soldering Iron and Fine Soldering Alloy Wire to set the Redstone Output of a controller block to strong. When set to Strong it can go through a casing, but not hatches, buses, or other blocks. Use it with an activity detector if you want to receive a signal from the controller rather than turning it on/off with one.  With a Machine Controller Cover, you can use redstone to turn the machine on and off. This is handy if you don't want to use a Soft Hammer to disable a machine yourself constantly.  The best way to do that is with an RS latch. One input will turn the machine on, and one input will turn the machine off. Use a comparator attached to a valve to measure the steam level in your multiblock Iron or Steel Tank. Invert the signal to turn on your boiler when the steam level is low. When the steam level signal is high, turn your boiler off. Use a trail of redstone to lower the signal strength. Because of boiler/turbine warmup, try to set the ON strength to 30 percent of capacity and OFF strength to about 90 percent.  Red alloy wire will hold a redstone signal for a longer distance than normal redstone, while redstone conduits will go indefinitely. You can also use amplified redcrystal to improve the strength of the signal for a long distance, repeating as necessary.  You can use an Energy Detector Cover in Normal Electrical Storage (including batteries) mode to monitor the power in a battery buffer and use it to enable/disable generators in a similar way. Change mode using a screwdriver. If you use a dense redcrystal, it will only allow signal through at a certain level, perfect for automating this kind of thing.  For a video version, check out this video: [url]https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jPfnifJEO64[/url]

### [-6057528648465876031] §5§lBuild Your Own Keep Inventory
The Ender IO mod adds one of the handiest enchantments in the game: Soulbound. An item enchanted with it stays in your inventory when you die, while everything else drops into your grave.  The best part: it works on almost anything, even items that normally reject enchantments:  - Backpacks (both normal and adventure backpacks) - including everything inside them - The Mantle of the Raven and the Rod of the Skies - Even Tinkers' Construct tools, which normally ignore vanilla enchantments completely  How to get it:  1. The Ender IO Enchanter crafts soulbound books from a Book and Quill and an Ender Crystal. See the [questlink]AAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAqg==[/questlink] quest.  2. Loot bags regularly drop enchanted books, soulbound among them - higher tiers give better chances. See the "Do You Feel Unlucky?" quest.  3. Found an item that already has soulbound? Strip it off with the Draconic Evolution Disenchanter to get the book. See the [questlink]AAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAIFA==[/questlink]quest - surrounding the Disenchanter with bookcases reduces the damage it deals.  Applying it is easy: use a vanilla anvil, just like any other enchantment.  [note]PS: Put soulbound on your backpack itself - the backpack and everything inside it stays with you on death.[/note]

### [2831] Covering Up
This quest goes over all of the covers, just in case you forgot or they weren't mentioned. If they have their own quest, look at it for more info. You can put them on basically any GT block, pipe, machine, tank, etc. Though that doesn't consider whether it will work right or be useful. Shift-right-click will open a GUI for many of them. For covers that you can adjust the amount of, clicking the left side with a screwdriver goes down, and right goes up. All covers block rain on the side they're attached to. Generally you can adjust covers with a screwdriver, whether they have a GUI or not. You can remove them with a crowbar if you forgot.  §lFluid-Related Covers:§r Pump: Transfers fluids at the speed specified on the tooltip.  Fluid Regulator: Regulates the rate that fluid can go through it, as well as pumping it. Can work x/s or x/t.  Steam Valve: Moves more, but is only for steam. Only goes to IV. Does not work on SH steam.  Overflow: Voids anything over the specified amount in the tank.  Fluid Detector: Will emit a redstone signal (RS) based on fluid fill rate of the tank it's on. By default, empty=15, full=0. Use inverted mode to swap. Useful on tanks and hatches.  Ender Fluid Link: Cover version of ender tanks to TP fluid around. Doesn't connect to ender tanks though.  Drain Module: Protects from rain on that side, and will fill the tank with water from rain if it can. Useless.  Fluid Filter: Filters fluid going through it. Click with a cell to set it.  Shutter: Blocks items/fluids when active. Use it with a machine controller and RS. Does not visually disconnect/block. You place it between the pipes, and put the MC on the same pipe.  §lItem-Related Covers:§r Conveyor: Sends items. Use an item pipe in import mode or on a GT block/machine.  Robot Arm: Sends from a specific slot in the machine it's on (internal) to another specific slot in a block next to it (adjacent). Keep going through numbers until you get it right, it seems to change per machine. If the output slot is full, it won't output. There needs to be a valid block with slots to send to, or the settings won't save.  Item Detector: Will emit RS based on item fill rate of the block it's on. By default, empty=0, full=15. Detects by filled slots, so doesn't work right with the super/quantum chests. Probably better for buses.  Filtered Conveyor: Filters up to one type of item (whitelist/blacklist) and acts as a conveyor module. Locked to either export or import.  §lEU-Related Covers:§r Energy Detector: Will emit RS based on EU fill rate of the batbuff/tank it's on. By default, empty=0, full=15. Make sure to set it to include batteries. Can also detect steam for some reason.  Power Pass Upgrade: Read the tooltip, then ask Bart how it works, he didn't bother telling anyone. He'll probably just tell you the code is the best documentation, just like the Tectech guys. May not actually be finished/work, either.  Tesla Coil/Tesla Coil Rich Edition: Use with the Tesla Tower to send EU wirelessly or something.  §lOther Covers:§r Player Detector: Detects players. About 10 block range. [warn]Currently owner/other is inverted on non-pipes due to some bug or another.[/warn]  Activity Detector: Detects either recipe progress or idleness. 0%=0 RS, ~95%=14 by default. In idle mode, idleness is 15, and is 0 when running by default.  Machine Controller: Turns machine on/off based on RS. Disable machine will keep it off regardless of RS state. Safe Mode only works on MBs. If a MB runs out of power, it will normally turn off, but a MC will turn it back on, continuing to void stuff. This will set it to the disable machine state instead. You will also get a notification. [warn]Safe mode is currently broken?[/warn]  Needs Maintenance: Emits RS if it meets the condition specified. Rotor=turbine?  Redstone Transmitter/Receiver (In/Out): OK, these things are confusing, so listen closely. First, set a frequency, either by using the GUI, or using the original way: right-clicking it with an item. Empty hand = 0, other items = their item ID. Be careful not to reset it to 0 by accident. Honestly, just use the GUI. You can set it to private or not, it's the same kind of thing as EnderStorage, so you don't blare RS across the server. Channel state = RS level.  RT(O) sets the channel state based on the redstone level sent from outside to the specific side. You can TP an RS from this to the RR(O). RT(I) sets the channel state based on redstone level that would be emitted from the machine itself. [warn]Always sends 15, so it's useless.[/warn] RR(O) outputs redstone level based on channel state. You can TP RS from the RT(O) to here. RR(I) controls whether a machine can work based on channel state, just like a machine controller cover does. If it has 0 RS, it stays off. At least 1, and it turns on. You send the signal from elsewhere with the RT(O). RR(I) can also be used in conjunction with other covers whose states talk about conditional.  IC2 Heat vents: Speeds up machines a tiny amount? Also looks neat. TODO: someone please look at the code to see what it actually does.  Crafting Table: Adds a crafting table to the block you can use. Mostly cosmetic.  Planks/Plates/Foils: Blocks I/O on that side. Use a screwdriver to set allowed types of stuff through. Blocks everything by default. Set the output to another side first to avoid changing its settings too. You'll probably 'use' this by accident at least once.  Computer Monitor: Adds a visual look. Cosmetic-only cover.  Lens: Cosmetic-only cover.  You can also use AE2 facades as cosmetic covers.

### [-5376193048649833794] Lootbag Uptiering
In GTNH, the lootbags are quite useful tools for boosting your resources. There are a wide variety of lootbag types, and those types all have upgrade paths to get better resources.  There are two paths you can take when it comes to upgrading your lootbags, you can either enchant it with fortune, or you can upgrade the bag itself. Enchanting the lootbag with fortune will increase the chances of more rare drops being given to you on opening, while upgrading the bag itself will change the item pool to that of the upgraded bag. Notably, if you uptier the bag itself, it will cost 3 of the previous bag tier, so make sure it's worthwhile to upgrade before you do so!  §2If you're looking to maximize your advantage it's recommended you both uptier your lootbag AND apply the fortune upgrade.  §r [note]Be sure to use NEI to see all of this information. Use 'U' on your lootbag and you will be able to see the potential drops, the drop chances based on fortune levels, and you will be able to see recipes for uptiering the bag.[/note]

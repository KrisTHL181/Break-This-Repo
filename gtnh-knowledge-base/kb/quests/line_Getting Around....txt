### [1182] Fluid Loader and Unloader
If you plan to automate the oil transport from your oil field far away, you need a fluid loader and unloader. A locking track will stop the train as long as oil is loaded or unloaded.  [note]PS: You should probably use the Long Distance Fluid Pipeline once you can make it in LV unless you really love trains.[/note]  Full details on how to use these may require online research (GTNH changes the recipes, not normal Railcraft functionality), but here is a simple list of things to do:  1. Make a loop of track from your base to the oil well. 2. Place a locking track near the well, and put a fluid loader two blocks above it. The track should be in Locking Mode - use a crowbar to change it. 3. Use the tank car on the filter in the loader and set it to Immediate mode. 4. Connect the well to the loader as normal. 5. Put a fluid unloader under a locking track at your base, and set it the same as the loader. 6. Automate the locomotive similarly - a fluid loader above a steam locomotive set to manual and filtered to the locomotive.  Either you got it working, you need to do more research, you realized it moves 32 buckets a trip and decided to just walk, or you can go to Discord and ask why anyone would build a train.  Item loaders also exist, but the main use for them may be to refuel your locomotive.

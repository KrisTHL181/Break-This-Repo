### [767] §2§lAnalyzing the Soil
Tired of looking for ores by hand? Want to find underground oil that will last a long time? A seismic prospector will tell you the ores in the surrounding chunks in a (sorta) handy book you can read, and also the underground fluids beneath you. It doesn't need to be powered.  To determine which kind of fluid there is in the chunk you need to scan the soil by right-clicking an Advanced Seismic Prospector with 2 Powderbarrels, 4 Glyceryl Trinitate cells, 8 iTNT or 16 TNT. This one doesn't explode the user too on use. Features! There are four types of oil or natural gas for the overworld. See the quest in Tips and Tricks once you've made an oil drill.  1) Right-click explosives. 2) Use a single Data Stick to extract the data by right-clicking on it after the animation has finished. Each will hold ONE result. 3) Next you need to put the data stick in a GT scanner to have it analyzed. 4) Place the analyzed data stick in the bottom right slot of a printer and add 3 paper in the top left slot and make sure you have at least 144L squid ink in it. 5) Take the printed pages and combine it with a piece of leather in an assembling machine filled with at least 20 mb of refined glue.  [note]TADA![/note] Now you have a book with detailed info about the chunks you scanned. Visual Prospecting adds the information to your map the moment you make the book.  §2Data Sticks are not easy (or possible) to make right now, so do not lose them.§r You can put it in a crafting table to erase it when done.  Ore locations are given as the center of the chunk. Ores that are marginal in that chunk may not be found in the center but on an edge. Look at where ores are located and determine the center of the vein. At higher tiers, it scans more chunks for ores. If you just want to know about fluids, use the lower tier version so the book isn't as long.  Each underground fluid field is 8x8 chunks. This starts at 0,0 and continues for 8 chunks east and south, containing 64 chunks. Not all fields have anything in them though. Within the field the amount can vary +/- 25 percent. The amount reported in the book is the minimum and maximum per field across all chunks in the field. ### [1510] §2§lBiomes Everywhere, But Not the One You Want?
Tired of searching all over the map for that Bamboo Forest or Tropical Rainforest? Desperate for clay dust and need to find a Canyon or Mesa biome? Nature's compass will lead you to the biome you're looking for, if it's within its range.  The compass menu has the following functionality: * When Sorted By: Name, you can filter the list by name or its parts. The search is case-insensitive. * You can search by keys and their combinations. For instance, name: bamboo, climate: warm, bh: -1.8, tag: forest. All available keys are: name, climate [hellish, hot, warm, normal, cold, icy], baseheight | bh (float, can be negative), heightvariation | hv (float), rainfall | rain (float), temperature | temp (float), humidity [damp, normal, arid], tags.  Some things to keep in mind: * Biomes from other dimensions show up on the list, don't bother searching for those unless you're in that dimension. * In general, the full list has biomes that are not generated by Realistic World Generator, like Sunflower Plains. * Radius 10000 means it couldn't find the biome. In this case after the initial search, its menu will show only the biomes in the 10k radius. Use Shift+RMB to see the whole list again. * You can try again at a different location - the range is 10k from your current position. * Artificially created biomes count, so remove them if you want to find a natural one. * There's lots of different 'versions' of biomes under the same category, so if you want a tropical one for tropical bees, there are actually a bunch of places you can get it. * For those who don't mind the freeze when searching, the radius can be changed in a config. The HUD position can also be changed in the config.

### [2143] §2§lChoices, Choices
Need a map on fuel choices? Well, we don't have that, but we have suggestions. You can use Steam up to IV, but beyond MV it gets pretty painful. When you reach HV turn diesel or bio diesel into Cetane Boosted Diesel. EV introduces the Large Gas Turbine, Large Steam Turbine, Large HP Steam Turbine, and Large Combustion Engine, which are more efficient versions of the earlier ones. The first 3 require turbines.  You can also make Nukes, or Wind and Water Kinetics.  For low-power operations, or dedicated players willing to set up autocrafting, you can begin using Solar Panels at MV.

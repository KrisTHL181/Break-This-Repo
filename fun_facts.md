# Fun Facts That Are Definitely True

1. Octopuses have three hearts, and two of them stop beating when they swim. They just hate cardio.
2. A group of flamingos is called a "flamboyance", the only accurate name in biology.
3. Cats cannot taste sweetness, yet they still judge you for eating dessert.
4. Wombat poop is cube-shaped: natures own dice.
5. Sloths can hold their breath longer than dolphins. They are just saving it up.
6. The mantis shrimp has 16 types of color receptors. It sees colors we cannot even name.
7. Bees can recognize human faces. They are watching. Always watching.
8. Penguins propose with a pebble. The bar for romance is a rock.
9. Sea otters sometimes hold hands while sleeping so they do not drift apart. Nature invented relationship anxiety first.
10. Crows can recognize individual human faces and remember people who treated them badly. They keep receipts.
11. Bananas are berries, but strawberries are not. Botany has chosen violence.
12. Sharks existed before trees. They have been here long enough to be disappointed in all of us.
13. A snail can have thousands of tiny teeth. Slowly approaching, heavily armed.
14. Ravens can mimic human speech. Imagine hearing "hello" in the woods and discovering it came from a bird with opinions.
15. Goats have rectangular pupils, giving them a very wide field of view. Nothing escapes the goat surveillance network.
16. Butterflies taste with their feet. Every landing is also a restaurant review.
17. Some turtles can breathe through their cloaca. Biology looked at the normal respiratory system and decided to improvise.
18. Cows have close social companions and can become stressed when separated from them. Even cows have besties.
19. Male seahorses carry the developing embryos and give birth. Dad said, "Fine, I'll do it myself."
20. Wood frogs can survive with much of the water in their bodies frozen during winter. They basically turn themselves off and back on again.
21. A group of crows is traditionally called a "murder." Flamingos got flamboyance; crows got a criminal record.
22. Rats laugh when tickled, although the sounds are too high-pitched for humans to hear unaided. Tiny invisible giggles.
23. Elephants can distinguish certain human voices and show different responses depending on who is speaking. Caller ID, but enormous.
24. The fingerprints of koalas are remarkably similar to human fingerprints. Somewhere, a forensic investigator is having a terrible day.
25. Some species of ants farm fungi for food. Agriculture was apparently not our idea either.
26. Dolphins have individually distinctive signature whistles that function somewhat like names. They can basically yell "Kevin!" underwater.
27. Polar bears have black skin underneath their fur. Their hairs are largely transparent rather than actually white. The entire bear is an optical illusion with teeth.
28. An ostrich's eye is larger than its brain. No comment.
29. Axolotls can regenerate limbs and parts of several organs. They saw permanent damage and simply declined.
30. Pigeons can learn to distinguish visual categories, including different styles of paintings in experiments. Your local parking-lot pigeon may be an art critic.
31. Some spiders travel through the air using strands of silk, a behavior called ballooning. The sky contains spiders because apparently the ground was not enough.
32. Squirrels sometimes pretend to bury food when they think another squirrel is watching. They understand deception and use it primarily for snacks.
33. Orcas have distinct group-specific vocal traditions that are learned socially. Even killer whales have regional accents.
34. The immortal jellyfish, *Turritopsis dohrnii*, can revert from its adult stage to an earlier developmental stage under certain conditions. It discovered New Game+.
35. A blue whale's heart can weigh well over 100 kilograms. Finally, an animal that can truthfully say it has a huge heart.
36. Some ducks sleep with one half of their brain at a time while the other half remains alert. They invented multitasking and immediately used it for paranoia.
37. Capuchin monkeys have been observed becoming upset when another monkey receives a better reward for the same task. The concept of workplace inequality predates HR.
38. Parrots sometimes give their chicks distinctive contact calls that the chicks later use themselves. Even birds get assigned usernames at birth.
39. Horseshoe crabs are older as a lineage than dinosaurs. They survived multiple mass extinctions and still look like someone dropped a helmet on the beach.
40. There are fungi that infect ants and alter their behavior before killing them. Yes, zombie-ant fungus is real. No, this information will not improve your sleep.

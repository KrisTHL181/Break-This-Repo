### [-4631197653832881856] A Summary of Crossbreeding
When crops breed, the resulting stats are the average of the parents' stats plus a random value between -2 and 4.  Essentially, the variation in stats is a fixed (-2 -> +4). If all parents have fertilizer in them, stat loss is prevented in all cases (0 -> 4).   Deterministic breeding is similar to how bees work, where you must bring the right species together. Some situations require more than two parents.  [note]Advanced crops (§5MV§r and above materials) will require the breeding machine.[/note]  Most crops will also have access to breeding pools. Breeding pools are like a set of tags associated with each crop. When breeding, if none of the parents produce a deterministic mutation, but some share one or more breeding pools, a random mutation may occur. The resulting offspring will always be a species tagged with one of the shared pools.  [note]Once you've obtained your first 31/31/31 seed, it can be helpful to use these random pool mutations to collect many different types of crops.[/note]

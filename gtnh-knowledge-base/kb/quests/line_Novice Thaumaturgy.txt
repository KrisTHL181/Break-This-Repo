Nodes are especially vulnerable, becoming Tainted themselves if exposed to this twisted magic.  Destroying the tendrils stops the spread, but unless §oall§r are destroyed this is only temporary. A more effective solution is to destroy the Tainted Land itself, as outside this specific biome tendrils quickly starve and die off.  If you are §oexceedingly§r careless with your Crucible you can, in fact, create Tainted Lands. So you would be wise to try wasting as little Essentia as possible, and clean up any you do waste.

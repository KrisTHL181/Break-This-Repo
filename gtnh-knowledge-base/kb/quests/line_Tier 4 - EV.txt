### [2210] §8§lDealing with Radiation
Nuclear materials cause two types of radiation effects. One is a large number of standard debuffs. These can be removed the normal ways, with milk or Gaia Wart or other methods that imitate them.  The other effect is a special Radiation unique debuff. This gets applied the longer you hold onto the radioactive item. Severely radioactive items can apply minutes of this deadly debuff. Regeneration might delay the inevitable death, but most regeneration sources aren't fast enough or long enough to keep you alive. You can try using an Amulet of Remedium to get rid of the effects. You should have one from your Moon trip, if not you'll have to dive into Thaumcraft to make one.

### [3074] §8§lPower of the Sun from Nuclear
Your good old singleblock Molecular Transformer is gone. So, how do you obtain Sunnarium? Well, there are a couple of ways, but at EV you'll need to radiate Glowstone and convert it to Sunnarium.  The Glowstone rod will slowly charge up if you put it next to any fuel rod in your IC2 reactor. The exact charging speed is (number of rods adjacent) * (floor(hull temperature/3000) + 1) per second. A dual rod counts as 2 rods, and a quad fuel rod counts as 4 rods for this purpose. The Glowstone rod will neither emit heat, nor cause other components to emit more heat. You can treat this rod as reactor plating in the IC2 Reactor Planner.  As you can see, the speed is directly proportional to hull temperature and adjacent rods. At the very extreme, you can fully charge up a Glowstone rod in less than half a minute and output one Sunnarium every 10 seconds (on average, since cells aren't charged at the same speed due to their different positions), but that requires a very considerable amount of resources to set up. It also involves a vacuum freezer to cool down the coolant cells and running the reactor at 66000+ hull temperature. Balance your needs and your resource situation wisely.  The wiki has good advice for reactor planning: [url]https://wiki.gtnewhorizons.com/wiki/Nuclear_Reactors[/url]  Replace the Containment Reactor Plating with actual Glowstone rods and you should be good to go. You can also use fewer reactor chambers by using less plating. Enjoy!

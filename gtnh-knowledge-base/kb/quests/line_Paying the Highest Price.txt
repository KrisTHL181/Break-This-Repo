### [-8970426691467039793] Ritual of Gaia's Transformation
The §5Ritual of Gaia's Transformation§r is the Blood Magic way of changing biomes. It consists of 116 Ritual Stones, including the Dusk ones, and requires an §9Awakened Activation Crystal§r.  To use the ritual, simply activate it, and it will transform an area up to §221x21§r.  If you want to change the biome you get from the ritual, use Arcane Plinths. The ritual starts from 0.5 humidity and 0.5 temperature, then consumes the items placed on the plinths to increase or decrease those values. It also starts by assuming a range of 0.1 is acceptable in both directions.  The items that modify these values are as follows: - Sand -> Humidity -0.1 - Sandstone -> Humidity -0.2 - Netherrack -> Humidity -0.4 - Lapis -> Humidity +0.1 - Water Bucket -> Humidity +0.2 - Lapis Block -> Humidity +0.4 - Coal -> Temperature +0.1 - Coal Block -> Temperature +0.2 - Lava Bucket -> Temperature +0.4 - Snowball -> Temperature -0.1 - Snow Block -> Temperature -0.2 - Ice Block -> Temperature -0.4  Once you have used the items to reach your desired environment, the ritual will give you a biome from it. If there are multiple biomes with that humidity and temperature, you can use wool to determine the exact one, for up to 17 conflicting biomes. If the ritual cannot find a valid biome, it will default to Plains.  Some cool ones include, but are not limited to: - Sky (also known as End) -> Orange Wool - Eldritch -> Cyan Wool - Eerie -> Light Gray Wool - TF Forest -> Pink Wool - Sacred Springs -> Yellow Wool - Tainted Land -> Purple or Blue Wool - Moon -> 0% Humidity, No Wool - Mars Flat -> 0% Humidity, White Wool - Asteroids -> 0% Humidity, Orange Wool - Space -> 0% Humidity, Magenta Wool - Toxic Everglades -> 70% Humidity, No Wool  [note]For the biome change to show up properly on the client, you may need to relog.[/note]

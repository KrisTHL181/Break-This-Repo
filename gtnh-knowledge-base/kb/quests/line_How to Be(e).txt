### [1123] Alvearies
The alveary is an advanced variant of the apiary. The alveary allows for combs to be produced faster due to its natural speed increase to production (as well as the ability to add up to six frames via the Extra Bees frame housings) and due to its ability to be modified by other blocks.  It is possible to adjust the internal temperature of the alveary using the alveary heater or alveary fan in order to accommodate bees that require hotter or colder climates.  Certain bees will not produce their specialty items unless they are Jubilant. This requires matching their preferred Temperature/Humidity, without any genetic modifiers. Some bees also need a block of something beneath them as well.  It is also possible to use mutators to increase chances of mutation, alveary lighting and alveary rain shield to allow bees without the nocturnal and tolerant flyer traits to work during the night and rain, respectively, and the swarmer to use excess royal jelly to create princesses that last for a limited number of generations.  [note]If you're mutating bees that need a specific block in it, you need to place the required block under the middle bottom, and have the one directly above be a normal alveary block.[/note]

### [502] A New Home

### [2093] A Stim a Day Keeps the Doctor Awake
Supporting circuit boards that can hold 4 electron tubes, the Electrical Stimulator provides a variety of functions. Use a Forestry Soldering Iron on an Intricate Circuit Board to set four slots worth of electron tubes. The tubes' capabilities are listed here as percentages:  Iron ET -> +0.5 Production Diamantine ET -> +1.5 Production Apatine ET -> 1.5x Pollination (Max 5x) Obsidian ET -> 0.8x Lifespan Lapis ET -> 1.5x Lifespan (Max 5x) Blazing ET -> Simulates Hellish temperature Golden ET -> 1.5x Mutation Rate (Max 5x) Tin ET -> 0.4x Territory (Min 0.1) & -0.1 Production Bronze ET -> 1.5x Territory (Max 5x)  Genetic Decay is also increased by 50% for all of them. This affects how long until ignoble bees die off, so don't worry about it if your bees are pristine.  Remember that lifespan is represented by ticks, not time. You need a world accelerator if you want a lifespan that is really less than 1 tick.  [note]Breeder's Notes: 12x Golden ETs and 20x Obsidian ETs across eight of these will make a super breeding party alveary; it will spike your mutation chances while dramatically decreasing lifespan. If you have been using the oblivion frame in an apiary to shorten lifespan for breeding, then this *does not* replace that because you cannot use world accelerators in alvearies. So the shortest you can reduce a bee's life cycle to is 1 tick = 27 seconds. On the other hand, dramatically increasing the mutation rate in the alveary may be faster for crossbreeding.  Production Apiarist's Notes: Alternately, you can slam down three of these with: * 10x Diamantine * 2x Iron That'll take you right up to the production limit. Any higher and you risk your pristine princesses turning ignoble from overwork.[/note]

### [1516] Breeding Bees Into Oblivion
If bee breeding is a dice game, then these are the biggest factors you'd control to win:  1. Fertility: How many dice you can roll each turn. 2. Mutation Rate: How good of a roll you need to make. 3. Lifespan: How often you can play the game.  The Oblivion Frame dramatically helps with #3 by reducing the bee's lifespan to the point that you can play the breeding dice game in one "tick" of 27.5 seconds. A bee with the "Shortest" lifespan trait cycles for 10 ticks (275 seconds), so you'd minimally cut the cycle by a whole order of magnitude!  You can find Oblivion Frames in Stronghold libraries. This is how you can get them in the early game for very aggressive bee breeding. Later, you can make them from Dragon Eggs. These frames are very precious and you don't want their durability to run out. A typical repair method is to use a Thaumic Restorer on them.  §oWait, to find a Stronghold reliably, I need some Eyes of Ender, and that's an HV-tier Chemical Reactor recipe!§r If you happen to have a Large Chemical Reactor, remember that it overclocks perfectly, so you can crank er' up with a bunch of MV-tier Energy Input Hatches.  §nThe Oblivion Bee Meta §r The oblivion bee meta can be enough to breed most bees quickly. You could do the whole bee breeding quest line with it, but you can ultimately improve on it with Alvearies and genetic modification. What you need for this meta is:  1. An Apiary 2. An Oblivion Frame 3. Some way to repair the frame (typically: thaumic restorer) 4. A Beealyzer and a lot of honey  This is a hands-on process. You don't automatically send the princess and drones back through because you need to watch the oblivion frame's durability. You inspect all the offspring bees after each cycle and hand-pick which to try next after a pass through the Beealyzer.  You can accelerate the Apiary with a World Accelerator. [warn]Be sure to protect the World Accelerator and supporting equipment from rain![/warn] You can use glass or a breakable clear glass so the Apiary can still see the sky while the World Accelerator is protected. If you need to use batteries, you can use a State Cell to draw out a button pulse to the world accelerator so it runs juuust long enough for an accelerated cycle.

### [3284] IAADDS
Aka the Mega Apiary.  It has three primary modes (change with screwdriver) and two operation modes (change with shift+screwdriver):  INPUT MODE: Does not take power. Put your queens in the input bus to put them in the internal buffer.  OUTPUT MODE: Does not take power. Will give your bees back to the output bus.  OPERATING MODE - NORMAL: The processing time is 5 seconds. Uses 1 Amp LuV per queen. All bees are affected by 64x acceleration and 8 Production Upgrades as well as a Genetic Stabilizer Upgrade. The IAADDS also simulates a perfect environment for all bees.  You can provide royal jelly to increase the outputs. 1 royal jelly grants 5% bonus per bee. They will be consumed on each start of operation and be applied to that operation only. The maximum bonus is 200%. The normal bee stats still apply of course, though the IAADDS is able to provide a perfect environment for any bee.  OPERATING MODE - SWARMER: In this mode you can only insert 1 queen. The IAADDS will then slowly produce ignoble princesses for 100 royal jelly per operation. The base processing time is 1 minute but you can overclock this process. The base usage is 1 Amp IV. ### [2081] Welcome to Biology Class!
Binnie's Genetics mod will allow you to pick and chose traits for bees, trees and Botany flowers. But not for free! Doing this comes at a price. The research process can be easily automated. Gene modification and drone generation can be automated, but are slow and infrequent enough that you will probably just attend to them when you need them.  The general idea is to sequence a pile of leftover drones until you get all the traits you want from it. You'll then have those traits forever and can inject your own combinations of everything you've found into drone Larvae to make new drones that carry those traits. Larvae hatch only into drones. You don't get to directly hatch princesses and queens, so you have to worry about these new traits sticking across generations.  These genetic edits don't let you create new traits out of thin air. They just let you pick and choose what you have already researched, and that has to come out of bees you already have. You may use this to set up all your production bees through the rest of the game, but traits like 4x fertility also help with breeding.  You'll want to prepare a lab workspace for this. The actual machines won't take too much space. In fact, you'll probably wind up smacking a bunch of them against world accelerators, which will require direct contact due to using Tile Entity mode. What will end up taking up more space is any passive resource generation you prepare to make the process more hands-free.  You will need an alveary with a hatchery to produce Larvae of the base bee you want to modify.  These machines require RF power. It's recommended to provide power to an Ender IO capacitor and power these machines with RF instead of powering directly with GT EU. You can connect GT EU power up to these, but you run into strange consumption from amperage conversion glitches, or machines not getting power despite looking like they have proper cabling.
